edge-function.ts is included as the matching backend source; commit it too to keep the repository current. No database migration is required.

After the site deployment completes:
1. Refresh /admin and sign in.
2. Click DOWNLOAD RESULTS if you want a local copy of the old results.
3. Click ARCHIVE CURRENT POLL. This closes voting and preserves the matchups and votes.
4. Click CREATE NEW POLL.
5. Add 2–4 new games. Video preview links are optional.
6. Enter opening and closing dates in Eastern Time. Save as draft while preparing.
7. Set Status to active and SAVE POLL when ready to publish.
8. Confirm the public page shows the new matchups.

Saved polls lets you reopen earlier polls and download their results.
